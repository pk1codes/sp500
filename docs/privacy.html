S&P 500 board privacy

This site does not ask you to sign in, and it does not have an account for you.

The page shows a signed range for the next S&P 500 cash close, plus the latest S&P 500 and ES prints. Those prints are read from Yahoo through this site. Yahoo sees the usual connection details, such as an IP address, under its own policy.

The site does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or location. It does not show ads, and it does not sell data.
